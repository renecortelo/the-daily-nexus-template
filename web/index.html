ABOUT // DARIO NOVELLI

Your morning signal, in three forms

V4 WEB FOUNDATION

FROM NEWSLETTERS TO SIGNAL

I start with the newsletters you deliberately place in your chosen Gmail label. I identify the useful reporting, connect the relevant developments, and turn it into a clear morning brief. The goal is not to repeat your inbox: it is to help you see what changed, why it matters, and what deserves your attention.

GEN, PLAY, AND READ

In GEN, choose the label, hosts, editorial mood, and the sections that matter to you. In PLAY, listen to the finished episode, follow the transcript, and open its source references. In READ, scan the companion newspaper when a screen is more useful than headphones.

YOUR RHYTHM, YOUR EDITION

Generate an edition when you need one, or schedule recurring briefings around your day. Each run can use its own name, newsletter label, voices, tone, and section order, so a broad morning review and a focused specialist briefing can live side by side without becoming the same show in different clothes.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

I collect messages only from your chosen label; Google's read-only permission itself covers the mailbox. Selected reporting is processed by Antigravity on Google's service and, for cloud runs, temporarily on your private GitHub runner. Kokoro renders speech inside that runner, without a paid speech API.

Sign-out locks this console and clears its displayed private data. Schedules keep running independently. The podcast, paper and feed use unlisted links: anyone holding a link can read or listen without signing in, and downloaded copies are not erased by sign-out. Keep these links private. New favorites are session-only unless you choose to remember their settings on this device.
